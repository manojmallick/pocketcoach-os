## 2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Coaching Messages**: Conversations you have with our AI coaches (stored in our database)
- **Action Cards**: Tasks, goals, and completions you create or select
- **Personal Information**: Profile name, email (if provided during sign-up or support)
- **Payment Information**: Subscription choices and purchase history (managed by RevenueCat and Google Play)

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Identifier**: Unique device ID used to identify you in our system (generated via UUID)
- **Usage Data**: Features accessed, frequency of use, time spent in app
- **Crash and Error Data**: With your consent, we collect crash logs and error reports to improve stability
- **Streak and Progress Data**: Daily completion status, session counts, action card completion rates
- **IP Address and Device Info**: OS version, device model (for debugging purposes)

### 2.3 Child Safety

PocketCoach OS is not intended for users under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information and take appropriate steps to comply with applicable law.
